⚓ Suggested Radius from Scope
Water depth at high tide (ft)
Rode deployed (ft)
Boat length overall (ft) — draws the hull on the map
Safety margin (ft)
Radius = √(rode² − (depth + bow roller)²) + margin. That square root is how far the bow can get from the anchor with the chain pulled straight — the true worst case, since catenary only ever holds you closer. Boat length is deliberately not in here any more: the alarm measures to the bow, so adding a hull length would count it twice. The margin covers GPS scatter and a little anchor resetting; 25 ft is sensible in normal holding. Widen it for poor holding or a crowded anchorage, and always confirm against how she actually settles after a full swing.
